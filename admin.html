Export Website

Download your updated website file to publish live.

How it works

1. Add all your products and update settings here.
2. Click "Download Website" below — it generates your updated index.html with all your products baked in.
3. Upload the new file to Netlify (just drag & drop to replace the old one).
4. Your live website is updated instantly. ✨

Publish to Netlify

Go to netlify.com → your site dashboard → Deploys tab → drag your downloaded file onto the deploy area. Done!

No coding. No confusion. Takes 30 seconds.
